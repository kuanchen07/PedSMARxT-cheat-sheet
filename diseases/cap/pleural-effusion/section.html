Drain if: respiratory compromise, O2 requirement >2L or escalating, mediastinal shift, growing effusion on US, complicated appearance on US

Drain management (doctor/surgery)

Simple effusion: observe for decrease in 24h → remove tube when <1–2 cc/kg/day with symptomatic improvement

Complex effusion (purulent or pleural WBC >10K): start tPA Q24h ×3 doses, CXR after

If no improvement after tPA: continue up to 6 doses, reposition tube, or consider VATS
