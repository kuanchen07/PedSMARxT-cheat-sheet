Pattern
Necrotizing fasciitis / Fournier’s gangrene / Ludwig’s angina

Deep tissue culture + susceptibilities
Blood culture ×1 (×2 if feasible)
ID consultation (strongly recommended)
Surgery consultation immediately

If >20% BSA → consider burn unit referral.
