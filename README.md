# PedSMARxT cheat sheet

**Live sheet:** [https://kuanchen07.github.io/PedSMARxT-cheat-sheet/](https://kuanchen07.github.io/PedSMARxT-cheat-sheet/)

A single-file **PedSMARxT** quick reference for **pediatric skin and soft tissue infection (SSTI)** care: procedures (I&D, cultures), empirical and definitive therapy, and pediatric dosing in one printable-style layout.

## Contents

This list tracks what the sheet covers; **content is updated often** as the cheat sheet changes.

The page is organized into five main areas:

1. **Purulent cellulitis — impetigo / ecthyma / folliculitis**  
   Folliculitis, limited impetigo, and ecthyma or extensive impetigo: when I&D and cultures are *not* indicated, topical vs oral options, penicillin-allergy alternatives, and vancomycin when systemically ill.

2. **Purulent cellulitis — furuncle, carbuncle, abscess**  
   **Mild / moderate / severe** pathways: I&D; wound and blood culture as indicated; I&D-only discharge for mild; TMP-SMX and doxycycline for moderate (MRSA coverage); IV vancomycin and culture-directed IV options (e.g. nafcillin, cefazolin, penicillin G) for severe; **step-down oral** choices by organism (MRSA, MSSA, GAS).

3. **Non-purulent cellulitis**  
   **Mild** (outpatient oral beta-lactams and PCN-allergy clindamycin), **moderate** (IV cefazolin, nafcillin, or vancomycin for type I PCN allergy), and **severe** (blood culture; empirical vancomycin; culture-guided definitive therapy via in-page jumps to **Necrotizing and toxin-mediated cellulitis** Table 4 and Table 5 pathway cards; ID/surgery notes).

4. **Necrotizing and toxin-mediated cellulitis**  
   Two emergent pathways in **side-by-side column cards**, each labeled (**Necrotizing fasciitis / Fournier's gangrene / Ludwig's angina** vs **Toxic shock syndrome / Staphylococcal scalded skin syndrome / toxin-mediated disease**), under one main heading. NF/Fournier/Ludwig: emergent debridement, deep tissue and blood cultures, ID and surgery; empirical pip/tazo plus linezolid (or vancomycin plus cefepime, metronidazole, clindamycin); culture-guided definitive regimens by organism (e.g. penicillin/clindamycin, MRSA options, Vibrio/Aeromonas). TSS/SSSS/toxin-mediated: blood cultures, ID and surgery; empirical nafcillin plus linezolid; culture-guided GAS, MSSA, and MRSA pathways.

5. **Preseptal / orbital cellulitis**  
   **Preseptal** (optional ophtho/ID as indicated; sinus/dental/unclear IV amp-sulbactam vs PO amo/clav (max 1200 mg) vs allergy doxy; clear skin IV vanc vs PO TMP-SMX vs PO doxy; routine I&D/cultures not indicated), **orbital** (admit CT with contrast; blood cultures; MRSA **nares PCR**; ID/ophtho and conditional ENT/OMFS &mdash; no IC: IV amp-sulbactam, oral step-down ≥72 h with ID; intracranial/abscess triple ceftriaxone + metro + vancomycin per ID), and **unclear** preseptal vs orbital (ophthalmology; consider CT; proceed per findings). **Bites & pyomyositis** (two column cards below): **human/cat/dog bites** &mdash; pre-emptive prophylaxis within 24 h when indicated (PO amo/clav vs anaphylactic PCN doxy); **infected bite** (ID/tetanus/rabies considerations; PO amo/clav Q8h or IV amp-sulbactam; PCN allergy doxy); **pyomyositis** &mdash; blood cultures ×2, MRI, surgery and early drainage; empirical vancomycin; culture-guided MSSA/MRSA/GAS IV options; preceding trauma links to **necrotizing fasciitis** pathway; consider oral step-down when clinically appropriate.
