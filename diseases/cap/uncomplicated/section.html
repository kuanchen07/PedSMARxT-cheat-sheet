Uncomplicated CAP*
* = section needs review
Severity
Mild

Outpatient — no systemic signs

Procedures
Chest X-ray (not indicated in typical viral presentation)
Blood culture (not needed)
Procalcitonin (only if antibiotics being considered and lab draws planned)
Treatment (oral, discharge)
PO Amoxicillin
30 mg/kg/dose TID (max daily dose 4 g)
PO Clindamycin
Alt penicillin allergy: 13 mg/kg/dose TID (max 450 mg)
PO Doxycycline
Alt penicillin allergy: 2.2 mg/kg/dose BID (max 100 mg)
Duration: 3 days (max 5)
Lab

No culture needed. Discharge on oral therapy.

Severity
Moderate

Requires admission, parenteral therapy

Procedures
Chest X-ray (only if significant hypoxia or respiratory distress)
Blood culture (not needed)
Procalcitonin (consider if antibiotics being initiated)
Empirical (IV while admitted)
IV Ampicillin
75 mg/kg/dose Q6h (max 2 g)
IV Ceftriaxone
Alt non-anaphylactic PCN allergy: 50 mg/kg/dose Q24h (max 2 g)
IV Clindamycin
Alt anaphylactic PCN allergy: 13 mg/kg/dose Q8h (max 600 mg)
IV to PO transition

When afebrile ≥24h, tolerating PO, clinically improving

PO Amoxicillin
30 mg/kg/dose TID (max daily dose 4 g)
PO Clindamycin
Alt penicillin allergy: 13 mg/kg/dose TID (max 450 mg)
Duration: 3 days total IV + PO (max 5)
Severity
Severe / Complicated

Effusion, empyema, sepsis, respiratory failure

Procedures
Chest ultrasound (diagnostic of choice for effusion/empyema)
Blood culture (required)
MRSA nares PCR (obtain before antibiotics — used for de-escalation, not escalation)
Sputum culture (if old enough to produce)
Pediatric ID consult (strongly recommended)
Pediatric Surgery consult (strongly recommended)
Wound culture (not indicated)
Empirical (IV)
IV Ampicillin/Sulbactam
75 mg/kg/dose Q6h (max 2 g ampicillin component)
IV Ceftriaxone
Alt non-anaphylactic PCN allergy: 50 mg/kg/dose Q24h (max 2 g)
Add if (septic shock, influenza positive <14 days, or concern for abscess)
IV Vancomycin
Pharmacist to dose
OR
IV/PO Linezolid
<12 years: 10 mg/kg/dose Q8h (max 600 mg) — ≥12 years: 10 mg/kg/dose Q12h (max 600 mg)

Discontinue anti-MRSA therapy if MRSA nares PCR negative

Duration: 10 days total IV + PO (longer in select cases per ID)
